# A four-minute demonstration

Use fictional information only. Start the local app with `Start Wanees.command`.

1. **0:00–0:35 — Meet Wanees.** Open `http://127.0.0.1:5180/?hospital=al-bahar&pathway=routine-xray&lang=ar`. Identify Al Bahar as fictional. Turn the companion with the pointer. Say: “A child can explore before signing in.”
2. **0:35–1:20 — Understand the visit.** Open the X-ray journey. Move through arrival, the imaging room, positioning and the picture. Explain that the words and models are draft content awaiting hospital review, and that the child's own care team gives the instructions.
3. **1:20–1:55 — Explore.** Open the illustrative tour, change reception to X-ray room, then inspect a thermometer or stethoscope. Call it an illustrative environment, not a real hospital photograph.
4. **1:55–2:20 — Take a pause.** Open Breathe with Wanees. Start the pacing visual, then stop it immediately. Explain that there is no required breath hold and no requirement to match the sample rhythm.
5. **2:20–3:10 — Express a preference.** Open the Comfort Passport interview. Choose “show me first,” a familiar toy, and time to answer. Show the caregiver review checkbox and print/download controls. Requests are preferences, not guarantees.
6. **3:10–3:35 — Switch language.** Switch to English. The current interview/visit progress remains. Show the caregiver area. If persistence is needed, begin a synthetic caregiver session before the passport flow and save a fictional note.
7. **3:35–4:15 — Explain review and evaluation.** Open Staff workspace, select Demo editor, save a short fictional draft and send it for review. Switch to Demo reviewer, approve and publish the demo version. Show the audit trail and evaluation questions. No effectiveness percentages or patient metrics have been invented.

## Reset

Public story: use Start again; activities have their own restart controls. Caregiver: export if desired, then use Privacy & data → Delete workspace to remove that synthetic session's records. Staff drafts remain in the demo store; to start a completely empty local installation, stop the launcher and move `local-data` to a backup folder. Do not erase a data folder containing anything the owner wants to retain.

## Honest answers

- “Is this a real hospital?” No. It is fictional and unpartnered.
- “Does it reduce anxiety?” That is a hypothesis for a properly designed study; no clinical effect is established here.
- “Does it book appointments?” No. These are personal notes and calendar exports.
- “Is the system production ready?” No. The delivery register identifies the remaining clinical, privacy, identity, database and operational work.
