# Softer character revision · 23 September 2026

The user found the first detailed characters scary. This revision prioritizes a gentle storybook appearance over realistic skin and prominent facial detail.

- Removed raised eye rims and separate cheek bulges from Amer, Maryam and the clinicians.
- Reduced eye projection and visible whites; increased the proportion of warm brown iris.
- Replaced the large nose/bridge and detailed nostrils with a small blended button nose.
- Reduced eyebrow weight, eyelashes and mouth contrast.
- Made skin, hair and shoes more matte; softened Maryam’s crimson fabric.
- Shortened and rounded Wanees’s muzzle, warmed the charcoal and ear colors, reduced brow weight and softened the stare.
- Retained the Omani welcome clothing, skeletal gestures, blinking, pause controls and 2D alternatives.

These are visual design changes. Appeal and comfort have not been validated in a study with children. The editable Blender files allow further art direction without replacing the application stack.
